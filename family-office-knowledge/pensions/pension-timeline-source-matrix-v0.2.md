## Reading rules

- `null + stable data_gaps` is the exact current output for **every** event below. A future runtime may emit only event-specific evidence after its separate approval; it may not promote screening or month-level observations to eligibility/requirements.
- “Eligibility/requirements” is not recognition. Recognition requires an individual decision; economic effective date requires applicable event-specific source/evidence; first cash payment requires proof that cash was actually available.
- No personal evidence belongs outside `family-office-workspace/`; all paths require human review. Annex VIII Part II `absence` or `not_matched` never implies eligibility, payer, amount, or payment.
