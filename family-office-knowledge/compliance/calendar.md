# Compliance calendar

## Scope

- Jurisdiction: Italy.
- Validity: 2026 only.
- Verified: 2026-08-29.

This note supports `it.compliance-calendar.2026.v1`. It records an operational reminder, not a determination of a household's tax obligation.

## Source

- Agenzia delle Entrate, 2026 IVAFE and IVIE calendar entries: the official calendar identifies 30 June 2026 for the balance payment and first advance payment. URL: https://www1.agenziaentrate.gov.it/servizi/scadenzario/main.php?chi=3991&come=514&cosa=11515&entroil=30-06-2026&op=4&vista=0

## Limits

The rule pack does not establish whether a person owes IVAFE or IVIE, whether a deadline is deferred, or who is responsible. Those facts require current official verification and professional review. Insurance, document, pension and family reminders are workspace-local facts and must retain their stated source or become data gaps.
