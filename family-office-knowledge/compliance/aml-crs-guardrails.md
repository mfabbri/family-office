# AML/CFT and CRS guardrail policy

## Scope

This note supports safety routing only. It does not determine whether a person,
account, transaction or intermediary is subject to a specific legal obligation.

## Verified public sources

- Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 is part of the EU AML/CFT framework for obliged
  entities. The project must not provide instructions to evade, conceal from or
  bypass AML/CFT controls. Source: `eu.aml.regulation.2024-1624`.
- The OECD Common Reporting Standard provides automatic exchange of financial
  account information and due-diligence/reporting procedures. The project must
  not assist practices intended to circumvent those procedures. Source:
  `oecd.crs.2025`.

## Operational consequence

Requests seeking concealment, false identity, reporting avoidance, bypass of
customer due diligence, or absolute anonymity are refused and escalated to an
appropriate compliance or legal professional. The guardrail reports its source,
limits and confidence; it does not make a filing, AML risk, residency, tax or
legal determination.
